# The ground was blurry, not the camera — 11 October 2026

## The number that settles it

The chase camera went from 640×360 to 1600×900 and the picture was *better
but still soft*. The camera was not the problem. Every world painted its
terrain with `grass_diffuse.png` across a 40 m tile:

```
256 px / 40 m = 6.4 pixels per metre
```

The chase camera sits 9 m from the aircraft, so the ground beside it was
being magnified about fifteen times. A sharper camera reproduces a blurry
surface more faithfully; it cannot invent detail that was never in the
texture.

```
ground_fine.png, 1024 px / 16 m = 64 pixels per metre
```

Ten times the detail.

## Generated, not downloaded

`tools/make_ground_texture.py` writes it. The alternative was a texture from
the web, which makes the look of every training world depend on a server
being reachable and a licence nobody recorded. A generated texture is
reproducible from this repository and diffable as the script that made it.

**It is made seamless on purpose, and checked.** A 16 m tile repeats about a
thousand times across a 16 km terrain, so a seam would read as a grid from
altitude. The noise is blurred *wrapped around itself* — tile 3×3, blur,
take the middle — rather than blurred as a square and left with mismatched
edges. The script then measures the wrap-around difference against the
difference between neighbouring rows inside, and fails if the seam is worse.

**The grain was tuned by looking, not by taste.** The first version came out
at a standard deviation of 5 grey levels, which at 64 px/m reads as clean
flat paint rather than ground. It now lands near 14 — grain visible from
nine metres without becoming noise — plus faint darker veins for the cracks
a dry lake bed actually has, and a little independent per-channel variation,
because a grey-scale grain over one flat colour looks printed.

## Why 16 m and not smaller

Smaller tiles are sharper and repeat more. A repeat that becomes visible
from altitude is worse than slightly softer ground up close, and fine
uniform grain hides its own repeat where a texture with any large feature in
it would announce it.

## Also fixed

**"No chase camera yet" was printing across a working chase camera.** The
fallback message sits in the same box as the picture and comes later in the
markup, so it painted on top of it. Put behind the image rather than over
it.

## Testing

`tools/sharpen_terrain.py` has the same safety as the other world tools:
PX4's copies are edited and this repository's carry the change as committed
content, backups to `.pretexture`, the result parsed as XML and restored if
it breaks, with `--dry-run` and `--revert`. Applied to all four heightmap
worlds; `tools/check_worlds.py` reports 5 worlds, 0 problems. flat_test is
skipped — its ground is a plane with a plain material, not a heightmap.

**Not yet seen in the simulator.** The arithmetic says ten times the detail;
only a look says whether the ground reads as ground.
