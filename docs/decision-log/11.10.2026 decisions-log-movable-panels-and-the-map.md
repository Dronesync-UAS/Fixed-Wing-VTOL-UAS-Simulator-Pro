# The rails became panels, and the map lost its rings — 11 October 2026

## Rails are panels now

Asked for: the side columns movable and resizable like the corner tiles,
and turnable into a horizontal bar placed anywhere.

A rail starts docked to its edge, where the layout switch owns its geometry.
Dragging its top edge undocks it: it keeps exactly the frame it already had
so it does not jump out from under the pointer, and from then on it has its
own header bar, a resize grip, and a button that turns it on its side.
**Reset panels** re-docks both.

**Turning swaps the long side with the short one.** A tall column turned
into a 148 × 744 bar would be a shape nobody asked for, so it becomes
744 × 148. Sections then run left to right with their buttons in a row, and
the bar scrolls sideways rather than down.

**The picture is only inset for a rail that is still docked.** Station
layout insets the hero so nothing covers it — but a rail the instructor has
dragged into the middle is no longer at the edge, and insetting for it would
leave a strip of dead space where it used to be. The browser test checks for
exactly that strip.

**Geometry is clamped server-side**, as the tiles are: a panel saved
off-screen or at zero size would be unreachable on the next load with no way
back but deleting the file.

## The map lost its range rings

Asked for: *"the map erase the rings only aircraft and home point and travel
path as standard uas map interface"*.

The concentric rings were drawn over the satellite imagery and are not what
a ground station map shows. Range and ceiling against their limits are read
from the CAP722B strip along the bottom of the console, which is always on
screen whatever the map is doing, so nothing was lost by taking them off the
picture.

**A scale bar stays**, because a map with no scale cannot be read. Two other
changes came with it, for the same reason the rings went — readability over
imagery rather than over a dark panel:

- the track is drawn twice, a dark casing under a bright line, so it holds
  up over both pale playa and dark terrain
- home is a ringed marker rather than a flat dot

## Testing

`tools/test_console_browser.py` drives the whole sequence in Chromium:
a docked rail, undocked by dragging its top edge, moved, resized by its
grip, turned on its side with the long axis swapped, its buttons still
reachable afterwards, no dead strip left in Station layout, and **Reset
panels** re-docking it. All pass, along with everything already there.

**Not yet used in a lesson.** Whether a floating instructor bar is better
than a docked column is a question only teaching with it answers.
